ℹ️ Skyjo Interactive Guide

1. The Goal & Card Colors (-2 to 12)

Just like Golf, the lowest score wins! Each player is dealt a 4 × 3 grid of 12 face-down cards and flips any 2 cards face-up to start.

2. What You Do On Your Turn

Choose ONE of two actions:

  • Take the face-up Discard card: Swap it with any card in your grid (face-up or face-down) and discard the old one.
  • Draw from the face-down Deck: Look at it. Either swap it with any card in your grid, OR discard it and flip one of your face-down cards face-up.

3. Special Trick: Clearing a Vertical Column!

If you get 3 identical numbers in a vertical column (all face-up), that entire column of 3 cards is immediately discarded for 0 points!

4. Ending the Round & The "×2 Doubling Trap"

When one player flips their last face-down card, everyone else gets one final turn. If you end the round, you must have the strictly lowest round score—otherwise your positive score is DOUBLED!
